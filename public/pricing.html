Frequently Asked Questions

★

How many businesses are on each postcard?

Each postcard features 4-6 businesses per zipcode. This keeps each listing prominent while keeping the cost shared and affordable.

How many households does each zipcode reach?

It depends on the zipcode — typically between 2,000 and 8,000 households. We'll give you exact numbers for your target area during the application process.

Can I target multiple zipcodes?

Absolutely. Each additional zipcode is $299/month. Many of our heroes reach 2-3 adjacent zipcodes for maximum neighborhood coverage.

Is there a contract or commitment?

No contracts. Cancel anytime. We recommend at least 3 months for best results — repetition builds recognition — but you're never locked in.

Do I need to provide my own design?

Nope. Our design team handles everything. Just send us your logo, a photo if you have one, and the info you want featured. We'll send you a proof before printing.

When do postcards go out?

We operate on a monthly cycle. Postcards are designed in the first week, printed in the second, and delivered by USPS in the third and fourth weeks of each month.
