How It Works

Simple, powerful, local advertising that reaches every door.

What is EDDM?

★

Every Door Direct Mail (EDDM) is a USPS service that delivers mail to every address on a postal route — no mailing list required. That means your advertisement reaches every single household and business in a targeted area. No one gets skipped. No one opts out. Every door.

Local Heroes leverages EDDM to create beautiful, high-quality postcards featuring the best local businesses in each Houston zipcode. Instead of paying for a full solo mailer, you share space on a curated card alongside other top-quality businesses — making it affordable and effective.

Why Physical Mail Still Wins

★

90% Open Rate

Direct mail has a 90% open rate compared to just 20% for email. Your postcard gets seen.

17 Days Average Lifespan

The average piece of direct mail stays in a home for 17 days. An email gets deleted in seconds.

No Algorithms

No pay-to-play feeds. No algorithm changes. No blocked ads. Physical mail reaches the physical mailbox, period.

Trust Factor

76% of consumers trust direct mail when making purchase decisions. It feels real because it is real.

Local Impact

People pay attention to what's happening in their neighborhood. A local postcard hits different than a Facebook ad from who-knows-where.
